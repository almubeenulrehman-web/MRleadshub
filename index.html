About Me

A specialist, not just a tool

I am a B2B lead generation & prospect research specialist. I help startups, agencies, SaaS companies and B2B businesses grow faster through targeted lead lists, verified data and personalised outreach — so your sales team spends time closing, not searching.

My rule is simple: no unverified lists. Every prospect is researched against your ideal customer profile, every contact is checked before delivery, and every message is personalised — because outreach fails on the list and the targeting long before it fails on the message.

  • Manual, human-verified research
  • Ideal customer profile (ICP) targeting
  • Decision-maker identification
  • Personalised outreach sequences
  • Clean, structured data delivery
  • Transparent reporting
